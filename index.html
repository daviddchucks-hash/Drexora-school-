Our Features

Everything You Need in One Portal

Our school management portal brings together all the tools students, parents, and teachers need.

Student Dashboard

A personalized dashboard showing your profile, passport photo, class, and important academic information at a glance.

Academic Results

View your term results with detailed subject scores, grades, positions, and teacher comments — any time, any session.

PDF Report Cards

Download a professional, printable PDF report card for any term — formatted exactly like an official school report card.

School Announcements

Stay up to date with all school announcements, events, and important notices posted by school administration.

Admin Control Panel

Administrators can manage students, upload results, reset passwords, and post announcements from a dedicated panel.

Secure & Private

Built on Firebase — each student sees only their own data. Industry-standard authentication keeps your information safe.

Mobile Friendly

Fully responsive design works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops — no app download required.

Dark Mode

Switch between light and dark themes for comfortable viewing in any environment, at any time of day.
